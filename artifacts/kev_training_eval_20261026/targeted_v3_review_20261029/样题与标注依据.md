## 1. probability / conditional_table

ID：`targeted-v3-review-20261029/probability/review/000`

```text
A return audit counts every item once.
| Depot | Returned | Kept |
| North | 18 | 42 |
| South | 54 | 36 |
```

**north_given_return (choice)**：Choose uniformly among all returned items. What is the probability it came from North? Round to two decimal places in percent.

- a: 25.00%
- b: 0.00%
- c: 50.00%
- d: 75.00%

正确标签：`a`；含义：25.00%

**return_given_north (choice)**：Choose uniformly among North items. What is the probability of a return? Round to two decimal places in percent.

- a: 0.00%
- b: 70.00%
- c: 30.00%
- d: 25.00%

正确标签：`c`；含义：30.00%

标注依据：正向以 North 总数为分母：18/(18+42)=30.00%。反向以全部退件为分母：18/(18+54)=25.00%。两问不能交换分母。
